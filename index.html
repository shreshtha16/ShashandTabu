Muskan, I’m really looking forward to all the little plans we keep making.

Our movie dates that somehow take more planning than the movie itself, eating momos together, and those peaceful lakeside walks on the weekends when we can just forget about everything else for a while.

And whenever we finally decide to go, I’ll be waiting for you on my Bullet. Just one request — please wear your jhumka. I have a feeling you’ll look way too beautiful, and I’ll probably forget where I was supposed to take you. ❤️

These may sound like small things, but honestly, these are the moments I’m looking forward to the most. The simple plans, the spontaneous ones, and all the little adventures that will slowly become our memories.
